Research in progress

Research overview

My ongoing research also includes a project funded by the Foundation for Economic Education (LSR 2025, Application No. 32-16490), which investigates the role of artificial intelligence (AI) and cybersecurity risks in corporate financing decisions using machine learning and computational text analysis. As part of this project, I have developed firm-level measures of AI adoption and cybersecurity risk by systematically extracting and analyzing information from corporate annual reports of companies across 91 countries. These measures provide a foundation for examining how emerging technologies and cybersecurity-related risks influence firms’ financing decisions and capital structures in both developed and emerging markets.
