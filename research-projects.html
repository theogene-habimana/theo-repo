Research and Projects

My ongoing research includes a project funded by the Foundation for Economic Education (LSR 2025, Application No. 32-16490), which investigates the role of artificial intelligence (AI) and cybersecurity risks in corporate financing decisions using machine learning and computational text analysis. I have developed firm-level measures of AI adoption and cybersecurity risk from corporate annual reports of companies across 91 countries, as a foundation for examining how emerging technologies and cybersecurity-related risks influence financing decisions and capital structures in developed and emerging markets.
